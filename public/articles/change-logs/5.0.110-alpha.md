Welcome to our biggest update EVER aka the final preview update. OpenProfile Preview is now all polished to look and function similar to the upcoming offline Windows application that will be releasing in a few weeks. Apart from that, we now have a ToS and Privacy Policy as well as a new license. As off this update, OpenProfile is no longer GNU but recognized as fully licensed and protected application under the OpenProfile brand basically meaning we're limiting rights towards our code. With that out of the way, we'll work on the next phase of the application over the course of the next year. Have fun and stay creative, authors!
