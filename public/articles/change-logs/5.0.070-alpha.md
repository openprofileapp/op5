OpenProfile's pages just went dynamic as .pge files! What does this means?? Well, you can now make your own .pge files using the provided format in the files and place them in the 'pages' folder for them to generate in your profile. Currently, the front page is not dynamic and if one page is removed from the folder, the app MAY not work as intended.  Ensure all page numbers and box ids are UNIQUE and that page numbers follow each other and are not skipped or gapped.
