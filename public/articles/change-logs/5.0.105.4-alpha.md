Hey everyone! Finally the long awaited online saving update is HERE! Along side that we have some news that a lot of people will enjoy, more on that later! It's crazy to think the application is now technically "completed". Sure, we have a LOT of fixing and feature adding to make but like, this is it. That's OpenProfile at it's bare bones!

Starting off with the online saving update, we weren't planning on adding this quite yet but we managed to solve the obstacles preventing us this! Unfortunately due to privacy reasons, we can't enable private profiles since they are running on an open API once saved to our servers, but in the future we will change this. If you really need to work in private then download your profile to your device then upload it back when you need to edit it again.

Secondly, we are announcing a new account type called "Early". All current accounts and new ones registered during the preview stage, will be granted an early account which will be equipped with  life-time premium. While it may not sound like much right now, in the future it might just be very well worth it!

Enjoy the update! Don't hesitate to email "support@openprofile.app" or join our Discord (discord.gg/w6aV9gkz8g) if you need support or to report a bug. 

PS: We have been made aware that OpenProfile is currently incompatible with Chromium. This may also affect other browsers but we will look into it ASAP.
