+ added back profile manager

Save your profiles locally in your browser using the profile manager! It's still early stages and very much recommended to first download profiles to your device before using it to save.

+ added galaxies

Galaxies or universes, however you wish to call them, are like profile category folder. They are meant for where the character origins from! Say you have a book named "Book" and another book named "Book II: Return of the Book" then you'd want to put all characters from each book in their respective places like the Book galaxy. Yeahh... it's also early development.
