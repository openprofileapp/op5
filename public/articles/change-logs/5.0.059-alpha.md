+ added quick share (ctrl + c)

Pressing ctrl + c when not editing a textbox will copy the entire profile's textbox data with an existing value on to your clipboard. Just press ctrl + v elsewhere for a quick and simple formatted profile data without exporting. Do note this isn't useable for importing back but can be useful to quickly share information without exporting and downloading.
