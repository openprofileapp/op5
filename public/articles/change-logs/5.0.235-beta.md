## Introducing bots on OpenProfile!
##### (limited to verified and partnered users upon request; only during development)

Soon, developers will be able to create bots to get data from their own or shared profiles for use on external applications and websites such as wikis and personal pages. Update profiles once on OpenProfile then watch their data automatically update to the applied websites. Profile owners will be in full control and can decide how authorized bots interact with their profiles and what they can see. Developers will also be able to give base permissions to their bots to limit how they interact on OpenProfile. You can download and view the node package using the link below!

https://www.npmjs.com/package/openprofile
