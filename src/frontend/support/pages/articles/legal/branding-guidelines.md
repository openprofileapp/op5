# How to use and refer to OpenProfile's brand (branding guidelines)
OpenProfile™ is proclaimed as the most advanced character profile template in the world and aims to cover every aspect of character creation through a transparent, source-available (coming soon) application that is free for everyone. Created by writers for writers, it seeks to make personal and commercial productions less painful through thoroughly organized profiles, as well as a public database for marketing purposes where fans can follow characters for updates and more.

The name OpenProfile comes from a combination of the words:
- **Open**: reflecting the application's origins as once fully open-source and its current source-available state.
- **Profile**: representing the characters that exist within the platform. 

Together, the name can also mean a character whose profile is open to the public, or simply existing within the OpenProfile platform.

## Trademark Usage
OpenProfile™ is a trademark of OpenProfile. The ™ symbol is optional when referring to OpenProfile in text. Use of the ™ symbol is recommended in formal or first-reference contexts, but not required in casual conversations or mentions.

### Correct Usage Examples
- "I have an account on OpenProfile™."
- "I have an OpenProfile™ on \<character-name\>."
- "Check out \<character-name\>’s OpenProfile™."

Avoid modifying the name or combining it with other words (eg: OpenProfiles, OP Profile, Open-Profile).

## Visual Identity
The primary and defining color of OpenProfile is:
- **Hex**: #CE1616
- **RGB**: 206, 22, 22

This color should be used whenever OpenProfile is visually represented, unless otherwise specified in official assets.

### Mascot
OpenProfile’s official mascot is Alice (she/her). She may appear in various forms and art styles across different application versions, but she should always remain recognizable and consistent with the official designs.

### Typography
The official typeface of OpenProfile is Alexandria. When Alexandria is unavailable, use a clean, modern sans-serif font as a fallback.

## Branding Assets
Official OpenProfile branding assets (logos, wordmarks, mascot illustrations, and related materials) are available for download:

- [Download Branding Assets](https://cdn.openprofile.app/beta/branding/press-kit.zip)

### Asset Usage Guidelines
These assets may be used without prior approval for the following purposes:
- Press articles, news coverage, and blog posts
- Social media posts and announcements
- Videos, streams, thumbnails, and presentations
- Community-made content referencing OpenProfile
- Educational or informational material about OpenProfile

When using OpenProfile branding assets:
- Do not alter colors, proportions, or orientation
- Do not crop, stretch, distort, or apply effects
- Do not obstruct or overlay other elements on the assets
- Do not present the assets in a misleading, deceptive, or defamatory context
- Do not imply endorsement, partnership, or official affiliation unless explicitly authorized

Commercial use, resale, or use as part of a product or service offering OpenProfile itself (or competing with it) is not permitted. If you are unsure whether a specific use is appropriate, contact OpenProfile Support at `support@openprofile.app`
